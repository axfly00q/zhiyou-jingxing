跟着“古城区域旅游导览图”，citywalk走起！

　　最近，苏州推出2024《苏州旅游导览》，整张导览共分为5个板块：苏州全域旅游导览、古城区域旅游导览、环金鸡湖旅游导览、环太湖旅游导览和环阳澄湖旅游导览。

　　其中，苏州古城区历史底蕴深厚文旅资源丰富，是市民和游客最喜爱的旅游目的地之一。

　　通过实地探访，我们在“古城区域导览图”上标注了古城区及附近的13处世界文化遗产点、232条街巷、8个A级景区、42处文博场馆、18座古典园林、63处文保单位、11处演出场馆、9个热门街区、6条历史老街、9座游船码头、17座桥梁以及酒店、购物点等场所。

▲古城区域旅游导览

　　古城内阡陌相通，河街相邻，古迹遍布，街市繁华，非常适合用citywalk的形式深入体验。根据“古城区域导览图”今天我们为大家推荐6个片区，规划了7条线路，行程紧凑不累，尽兴玩不无聊！

　　01.东北街片区

　　苏州市旅游咨询中心(东北街中心站)→苏州博物馆本馆→拙政园→狮子林→平江历史文化街区

　　这是一条比较热门的游玩线路。从苏州市旅游咨询中心(东北街中心站)出发，首先前往苏州博物馆本馆(需提前预约)。博物馆由世界著名华裔建筑大师贝聿铭设计，是苏州地标性建筑之一，也是来苏游客必打卡之地。

　　几步之遥就是苏州现存最大的古典园林——拙政园。拙政园(80元)是苏州园林夏日赏荷好去处，荷花亭亭出水，灼灼迎朝，凡步行之处，皆有莲香。

　　狮子林(40元)被誉为“假山王国”，以湖山奇石，洞壑深邃而盛名于世，绿树环抱，环境十分幽静。

　　平江历史文化街区为大运河苏州段七个遗产点之一，是苏州古城迄今保存最完整的历史文化保护区，被称为是“苏州古城的缩影”。街区内名人故居、旧时宅第、祠堂、博物馆比比皆是，还有许多苏式美食可以品尝。

　　逛累了，还可以去琵琶语评弹茶馆、翰尔园、伏羲古琴文化馆等茶馆，点一杯茶，听听苏州的昆曲评弹。

　　【小彩蛋：东北街片区小众线路】

　　苏州市旅游咨询中心(东北街中心站)→苏州园林博物馆→金谷里艺术馆→知止书店→北半园→东园(全程无需门票)

　　从苏州市旅游咨询中心(东北街中心站)出发，首先前往苏州园林博物馆。博物馆详细展示了叠山、理水、花木、建筑等造园手法，带你深入读懂苏州园林。

　　金谷里艺术馆原是吴县刺绣厂废旧厂房，改造后摇身一变成为新晋网红打卡地。博物馆“临一水繁华，揽一城风雅”，内有艺术展陈、酒店住宿、曲艺演出等多样元素。

　　到白塔东路的知止书店挑几本好书，接着过马路就到了北半园(书香府邸平江府酒店内)。半山半水半入园，半桥半亭半廊曲，园内布局独特，错落有致。

　　再往东就到了东园，园内绿树掩映，花开繁茂，散步休闲之余还可以喝茶。

　　02.西北街-桃花坞片区

　　北寺塔→苏州丝绸博物馆→苏州美术馆→苏州市名人馆→桃花坞历史文化片区(全程无需门票)

　　北寺塔高76米，是苏州古城区最高的建筑。塔八面九层、重檐复宇、朱栏萦绕、气势非凡。

　　苏州丝绸博物馆毗邻北寺塔，是我国第一座丝绸专题博物馆，内设有历史馆、现代馆、少儿科普馆、桑梓苑、丝织机械陈列室和钱小萍丝绸文化艺术馆六大展区。

　　苏州美术馆和苏州市名人馆都位于苏州市公共文化中心内。苏州美术馆内正在举办“日月同辉——清华大学美术学院陈辉教授水墨作品巡展”。苏州市名人馆集中展示了447位姑苏先贤，是苏州“物华天宝、人杰地灵”的精彩实证。

　　接着沿校场桥路一直走，可前往桃花坞历史文化片区。这里有唐寅故居遗址、唐寅祠、文昌阁等点位可以参观，还可以到苏州方志馆新馆，挑一本志书，了解苏州的历史。

　　03.阊门-山塘街片区

　　环秀山庄→艺圃→阊门→山塘街→虎丘山风景名胜区

　　环秀山庄和艺圃都是被列入《世界遗产名录》的苏州古典园林。环秀山庄(门票15元)的湖石假山为清代叠山大师戈裕良之作，虽由人作，有如天开。明代园林艺圃(门票10元)位于深巷之中，环境幽静，风格质朴，很多苏州人喜欢在水榭“延光阁”中喝茶赏景。

　　阊门是阖闾大城的八座城门之一。历史上，阊门为繁华富庶之地，与一河之隔的山塘地区和石路地区以水运为基础，成为城市商业中心。

　　山塘街东起阊门，西至虎丘，全长约七里，为唐代诗人白居易任苏州刺史时募工凿河堆堤而成。东半段是人气超高的游览区，商业繁华，热闹鼎沸;西半段安静从容，生活气浓。

　　虎丘(门票70元)被誉为“吴中第一名胜”，这里古迹众多、历史悠久，是一座生动的历史博物馆。山上的云岩寺塔是苏州古城的地标建筑之一，塔斜而不倒，雄伟古朴，屹立千年。

　　04.平桥直街-五卅路片区

　　柴园(苏州教育博物馆)→五卅路→皇废基→肯达美术馆→章太炎故居→中共苏州独立支部旧址(全程无需门票)

　　柴园是一座建于清代的园林，现为苏州教育博物馆，博物馆分千年薪传、百年启道、时代华章和杏坛英华四个主要部分，全面展示了苏州源远流长的教育史。

　　五卅路是一条因“五卅运动”而得名的道路，路东有体育馆、苏州公园和市文物保护单位金城新村，路西有体育场、同德里及同益里。路旁梧桐交柯，浓荫覆地，安谧清幽。

　　皇废基小巷中有清新可爱的涂鸦和满墙凌霄花，很适合打卡拍照。巷口的肯达美术馆里常常举办各种展览和沙龙活动。

　　章太炎故居为江苏省文物保护单位，内有章太炎故居展陈、古吴轩书店章园店、咖啡馆和自习室，不定期举办各类展览和讲座。

　　附近的中共苏州独立支部旧址(原苏州乐益女子中学)，是苏州历史上第一个真正发挥作用的党组织，揭开了苏州革命历史的新篇章。

　　05.双塔片区

　　苏州市旅游咨询中心(凤凰街中心站)→定慧寺→吴作人艺术馆→罗汉院双塔→双塔市集→甲辰巷砖塔→相门城墙

　　从苏州市旅游咨询中心(凤凰街中心站)出发，逛一逛定慧寺巷。巷子很短，却拥有始建于唐代的定慧寺、北宋的罗汉院双塔(门票8元)，以及小而美的吴作人艺术馆。

　　双塔市集是古城区的人气菜场之一，在姑苏原生民坊小食区域，你可以品尝到许多地道的苏州味道，香脆的鸡蛋饼，甜蜜的糖粥和小圆子，皮薄馅足的生煎包，糯叽叽的糕团点心......

　　附近的巷子里藏着甲辰巷砖塔，塔高6.82米，以清水砖砌成，不施粉彩，朴实无华，始建年代无考，其结构造型体现了较多唐末宋初建筑特征。

　　最后来到相门城墙。相门是阖闾大城的八座城门之一，城墙下还有一座苏州城墙博物馆可以参观。

　　06.十全街片区

　　苏州市旅游咨询中心(十全街中心站)→网师园→十全街→乌鹊桥路→颜文樑纪念馆→沧浪亭→可园→文庙(苏州碑刻博物馆)

　　从苏州市旅游咨询中心(十全街中心站)出发，第一站前往几步之遥的网师园。网师园(门票40元)布局精致，是江南中小型古典园林的代表作。

　　接着沿十全街漫步，街上有经营了几十年的正宗苏味和老字号美食，也有不断涌现的网红餐厅、时髦小店。从十全街拐进乌鹊桥路，还可以打卡“乌鹊桥”网红墙和“乐村”街区。

沧浪亭(门票20元)始建于北宋，为苏州现存诸园中历史最久，保持了建园时“草树郁然，崇阜广水”之特色，景色自然，建筑简雅。沧浪亭附近还有可园(门票25元)和颜文樑纪念馆可以参观。过个马路，再逛一逛相对小众的苏州碑刻博物馆，在一方方碑刻间品读苏州的往昔。

Explore Suzhou Old Town on a City Walk with the Official Tourism Map!

Source: Fun Suzhou | Time: August 21, 2024

Suzhou has recently launched the 2024 Suzhou Tourism Guide, which includes 5 sections:

Suzhou Regional Tourism Guide

Old Town Area Tourism Guide

Jinji Lake Area Guide

Taihu Lake Area Guide

Yangcheng Lake Area Guide

The old town of Suzhou is rich in history and culture, making it one of the most popular destinations for both locals and tourists.

Based on field research, the Old Town Area Tourism Map marks the following attractions:

13 World Heritage Sites

232 streets and lanes

8 A-level scenic spots

42 cultural venues and museums

18 classical gardens

63 cultural relics protection sites

11 performance venues

9 popular blocks

6 historic old streets

9 cruise terminals

17 bridges plus hotels and shopping areas.

With crisscrossing paths, riverside streets, and ancient sites everywhere, the old town is perfect for city walking. We have selected 6 zones and designed 7 routes — compact, easy, and full of fun!

01 Northeast Street Area

Suzhou Tourism Service Center → Suzhou Museum (Main Building) → Humble Administrator’s Garden → Lion Grove Garden → Pingjiang Road Historic & Cultural Block

This is one of the most popular routes.

Suzhou Museum was designed by world-renowned architect I. M. Pei. It is a landmark and must-visit spot (advance reservation required).

Humble Administrator’s Garden (¥80) is Suzhou’s largest classical garden, ideal for enjoying lotus flowers in summer.

Lion Grove Garden (¥40) is known as the “Kingdom of Rockeries,” with fantastic stones and peaceful surroundings.

Pingjiang Road Historic Block is a UNESCO Grand Canal heritage site and the best-preserved historic zone in Suzhou. It features former residences, old mansions, museums, and local snacks. You can also enjoy Kunqu Opera and pingtan storytelling in teahouses.

Off-the-Beaten-Path Mini Route (FREE)

Suzhou Tourism Service Center → Suzhou Garden Museum → Golden Valley Art Center → Zhizhi Bookstore → North Half Garden → East Garden

Suzhou Garden Museum explains classic gardening techniques: rock piling, water layout, planting, and architecture.

Golden Valley Art Center is a popular spot renovated from a former embroidery factory.

Zhizhi Bookstore on Baita East Road offers a quiet reading break.

North Half Garden has a unique layout: half-hill, half-water, half-bridge, half-pavilion.

East Garden is lush and quiet, great for walking and relaxing.

02 Northwest Street – Taohuawu Area (FREE)

North Temple Pagoda → Suzhou Silk Museum → Suzhou Art Museum → Suzhou Celebrity Hall → Taohuawu Historic & Cultural Zone

North Temple Pagoda (76m) is the tallest building in old Suzhou.

Suzhou Silk Museum is China’s first silk-themed museum with six exhibition halls.

Suzhou Art Museum and Suzhou Celebrity Hall are inside the Suzhou Public Culture Center.

Taohuawu Zone includes the former residence of Tang Yin (Tang Bohu), Tang Yin Temple, and Wenchang Pavilion.

03 Changmen Gate – Shantang Street Area

Huanxiu Mountain Villa → Garden of Cultivation → Changmen Gate → Shantang Street → Tiger Hill Scenic Area

Huanxiu Mountain Villa (¥15) features a masterpiece rockery by Qing-dynasty master Ge Yuliang.

Garden of Cultivation (¥10) is a quiet, simple Ming-dynasty garden.

Changmen Gate is one of the eight gates of the ancient city of Helü.

Shantang Street, built by Bai Juyi, stretches seven miles to Tiger Hill. The east part is lively; the west part is peaceful and residential.

Tiger Hill (¥70), known as the “No.1 Scenic Spot in Wuzhong,” is home to the iconic Yunyan Temple Pagoda.

04 Pingqiao Straight Street – Wusa Road Area (FREE)

Chai Garden (Suzhou Education Museum) → Wusa Road → Huangfeiji → Kenda Art Museum → Former Residence of Zhang Taiyan → Former Site of CPC Suzhou Independent Branch

Chai Garden shows Suzhou’s long educational history.

Wusa Road is quiet with phoenix trees lining the street.

Huangfeiji Lane has lovely graffiti and trumpet creeper walls.

Kenda Art Museum hosts regular exhibitions.

Former Residence of Zhang Taiyan includes an exhibition hall, bookstore, and café.

The CPC Suzhou Independent Branch is the birthplace of the Party organization in Suzhou.

05 Twin Pagodas Area

Suzhou Tourism Service Center (Fenghuang Street) → Dinghui Temple → Wu Zuoren Art Museum → Twin Pagodas of Luohan Temple → Twin Pagodas Market → Jiaochen Alley Brick Pagoda → Xiangmen City Wall

Dinghui Temple Lane includes a Tang-dynasty temple and Northern Song twin pagodas (¥8).

Twin Pagodas Market is a popular local market with classic Suzhou snacks: egg pancakes, sugar porridge, fried buns, and sticky pastries.

Jiaochen Alley Brick Pagoda (6.82m) is a simple, ancient brick pagoda.

Xiangmen City Wall is one of the eight ancient gates, with a city wall museum below.

06 Shiquan Street Area

Suzhou Tourism Service Center (Shiquan Street) → Master of the Nets Garden → Shiquan Street → Wuqueqiao Road → Yan Wenliang Memorial Hall → Canglang Pavilion → Keyuan Garden → Confucian Temple (Suzhou Stone Tablet Museum)

Master of the Nets Garden (¥40) is an exquisite example of smalltomedium Jiangnan gardens.

Shiquan Street has time-honored restaurants, local food, trendy shops, and cafés.

Canglang Pavilion (¥20), built in the Northern Song, is Suzhou’s oldest existing garden.

Nearby are Keyuan Garden (¥25) and Yan Wenliang Memorial Hall.

Confucian Temple (Suzhou Stone Tablet Museum) displays ancient stone steles recording Suzhou’s history.

苏州博物馆成立于1960年元旦，是收藏、展示、研究、传播苏州历史、文化、艺术的综合性博物馆。馆址太平天国忠王府为首批全国重点文物保护单位，是国内保存至今最完整的一组太平天国历史建筑物。

苏州博物馆藏品总数25786件/套，珍贵文物9742件/套，其中一级品222件/套，二级品831件/套，三级品8689件/套，以历年考古出土文物、明清书画和工艺品见长。

2006年10月6日，由世界建筑大师贝聿铭设计的苏州博物馆本馆建成并正式对外开放。本馆占地面积约10700平方米，建筑面积19000余平方米，以“中而新，苏而新”的设计理念，融建筑于园林之中，化创新于传统之间。加上修葺一新的太平天国忠王府，总建筑面积达26500平方米，与毗邻的拙政园、狮子林等园林名胜构成了一条丰富多彩的文化长廊。

2021年9月25日，苏州博物馆西馆开放试运行。西馆位于苏州市高新区狮山广场，由德国建筑设计排行第一的GMP建筑师事务所设计。建筑面积48365平方米，展陈面积13391平方米，展出馆藏文物2100余件/套，包括良渚时期玉琮、宋钧窑鼓钉三足洗、元代灰陶枇杷贡器、顺治款天女散花碗等珍贵文物。西馆建筑借鉴江南水乡街巷邻里格局，在传承经典的同时，彰显鲜明的地域特色。常设展厅有通史陈列馆、苏作工艺馆、探索体验馆、国际合作馆和苏色生活馆。

2008年5月，苏州博物馆成为首批国家一级博物馆，并对公众免费开放。近年来，苏州博物馆各项业务工作推陈出新，屡创佳绩，先后荣获“全国最具创新力博物馆”“全国文化系统先进集体”“全国文明单位”“首届全国博物馆志愿服务典型案例”“文物事业高质量发展十佳案例”“全国模范职工之家”“第三批‘数据要素×’典型案例”等荣誉，“衡山仰止——吴门画派之文徵明特展”“大英博物馆藏意大利文艺复兴时期素描精品展”“画屏：传统与未来特展”“纯粹江南 技忆苏州——苏州博物馆西馆基本陈列”和“亚述之王：亚述巴尼拔”特展先后五次获评“全国博物馆十大陈列展览精品”。2018年、2023年和2025年，苏州博物馆在“2014-2016”年度、“2019-2021”年度、“2022—2024”年度国家一级馆运行评估中连续三次获评“优秀”等次，稳居全国第一梯队。此外，2023年苏州博物馆成为中国博物馆协会青年工作委员会主任委员单位，以此为依托在行业领域更广泛地践行职能、提升文化影响力。

苏州博物馆以江南文化为核心展示内容，秉持“立江南，观世界”的办馆思路，着力打造“代表江南文化特色与风格的，引领行业创新发展的世界一流博物馆”，为观众传递苏式雅韵魅力，讲述江南文化故事，展现世界文明风采。

Suzhou Museum was founded on New Year's Day in 1960. It is a comprehensive museum dedicated to the collection, exhibition, research and dissemination of Suzhou's history, culture and art. The museum is housed in the Palace of the Loyal King of the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom, one of the first batch of National Major Historical and Cultural Sites Protected at the State Level, and the most intact complex of surviving buildings from the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom across China.

The museum holds a total of 25,786 cultural relics (sets), among which 9,742 are precious artifacts. The collection includes 222 Grade I cultural relics, 831 Grade II and 8,689 Grade III items. It is renowned for archaeological discoveries, paintings and calligraphy of the Ming and Qing dynasties, as well as traditional crafts.

Designed by the world-famous architect I. M. Pei, the main building of Suzhou Museum was completed and officially opened to the public on October 6, 2006. Covering a land area of approximately 10,700 square meters and a floor area of over 19,000 square meters, it follows the design philosophy of "Modern China, Modern Suzhou". The architecture blends harmoniously with classical gardens, integrating innovation into tradition. Together with the fully renovated Palace of the Loyal King, the total floor area reaches 26,500 square meters. Adjacent to the Humble Administrator's Garden, Lion Grove Garden and other scenic gardens, it forms a splendid cultural corridor.

The West Branch of Suzhou Museum launched its trial operation on September 25, 2021. Located at Shishan Square in Suzhou High-tech Zone, it was designed by GMP Architekten, Germany's top architectural firm. The branch has a total floor area of 48,365 square meters and an exhibition area of 13,391 square meters, displaying more than 2,100 cultural relics (sets). Highlights include a jade cong from the Liangzhu Culture period, a three-legged washer of Jun ware from the Song Dynasty, a grey pottery tribute vessel shaped like loquats from the Yuan Dynasty, and a bowl decorated with celestial maidens in bloom bearing the reign mark of Emperor Shunzhi. Drawing on the layout of streets and alleyways typical of water towns in the Jiangnan region, the building inherits classic styles while showcasing distinctive local features. Its permanent exhibition halls consist of the General History Gallery, Suzhou Craft Gallery, Interactive Exploration Hall, International Cooperation Gallery and Jiangnan Lifestyle Gallery.

In May 2008, Suzhou Museum was designated one of the first batch of National First-Class Museums and opened to the public free of charge. In recent years, the museum has made continuous innovations and achieved remarkable results. It has been awarded numerous honors, namely China's Most Innovative Museum, Advanced Collective of the National Cultural System, National Civilized Unit, National Model Case of Museum Volunteer Services, Top Ten Cases for High-quality Development of Cultural Relics Undertakings, National Model Staff Home, and one of the Third Batch of Typical Cases of Data Factor Application.

Five of its special exhibitions have been selected as Top Ten Outstanding Museum Exhibitions in China: Elevation of Hengshan — Special Exhibition of Wen Zhengming of the Wu School of Painting, Master Drawings of the Italian Renaissance from the British Museum, Painted Screens: Tradition and the Future, Essence of Jiangnan · Craftsmanship of Suzhou — Permanent Exhibition of the West Branch of Suzhou Museum, and King of Assyria: Ashurbanipal.

The museum was rated Excellent in the national assessment of National First-Class Museums for three consecutive cycles (2014–2016, 2019–2021 and 2022–2024) in 2018, 2023 and 2025, securing its position among the country's top-tier museums. In 2023, Suzhou Museum became the leading member unit of the Youth Work Committee of the Chinese Museums Association, enabling it to further fulfill its responsibilities and expand cultural influence within the industry.

Centered on Jiangnan culture, Suzhou Museum adheres to the mission "Rooted in Jiangnan, Embracing the World". It strives to build a world-class museum that embodies the characteristics of Jiangnan culture and leads innovative development in the museum field. The museum presents the elegant charm of Suzhou, tells stories of Jiangnan culture and displays the splendors of civilizations from around the globe.

History

Master Faqian: The Reviving Patriarch

Elder Faqian lived during the transition from the Northern to Southern Song Dynasty. According to Record of Fengqiao Temple by Sun Di, Fengqiao Temple (later known as Hanshan Temple) was damaged by government troops during the Jianyan reign (1127–1130). “Monks fled into hiding; fallen eaves lay on the ground, and broken tiles struck people.”

Master Daxiu: Poetry, Calligraphy, Painting and Zen

Master Daxiu, secular surname Yan, styled himself Drunken Chan and Stone Daoist. He was from Renshou, Sichuan. He practiced Taoism in childhood and turned to Buddhism as he grew older. As he wrote in one of his five-character poems: “At young age I loved immortality; when grown I devoted to Buddhism. Not until my fifties did I realize how hard it is to be a man.”

Hanshan: The Poet-Monk

The name Hanshan Temple is closely associated with Hanshan, the famous poet-monk of the Tang Dynasty. As recorded in Record of the Restoration of Hanshan Temple by Yao Guangxiao: “There lived a man named Hanshan… who came here and built a thatched hut to reside.”

Master Zhengxu: Building the Scripture Library

Master Xiaoshan Zhengxu was abbot of Hanshan Temple during the Jiajing and Wanli reigns of the Ming Dynasty. He vowed to raise funds and build the Dragon Cabinet Scripture Library.

Master Puming: The Founding Ancestor

Master Puming, secular name Fa Jing, surname Zhu, was from Kuaiji (present-day Shaoxing, Zhejiang). In his youth, he behaved differently from other children, often chanting “Amitabha.” He made fields of merit by piling sand and built halls and pagodas with artemisia, never speaking of worldly affairs, wholeheartedly seeking the Buddha Dharma.

Master Xingkong: Rebuilding the Temple

Master Xingkong, courtesy name Shengzhi, was born on February 5, 1922, to a devout Buddhist family in Zhangmu, Taizhou (now Jiangyan District), Jiangsu. Intelligent and fond of learning from childhood, he left home at 14 to become a monk at Guanyin Nunnery in Xijiazhuang of his hometown, receiving tonsure under Master Jing’an. At 19, he received full ordination at Nanchan Temple in Wuxi.

Shengu Chang: Carrying Forward the Legacy

In the third year of the Yongle reign, Master Shengu Chang was appointed abbot of Hanshan Temple by the Buddhist Records Office. Upholding strict discipline and composed conduct, he resolved to rebuild the temple.

Master Xiqian: Founding the Hall

Master Xiqian (700–790), also known as Shitou Xiqian, was a prominent monk of the Tang Dynasty, serving during the reigns of Wu Zetian, Ruizong, Xuanzong and Dezong.
