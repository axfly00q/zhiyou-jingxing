143,200 Foreign Visitors! A Year-on-Year Increase of 181.89% — Suzhou Classical Gardens Win Over Overseas Tourists Time: 2024-07-24 01:52 Source: Gusu Evening News

Reporters learned that since the beginning of this year, the Suzhou Municipal Bureau of Gardens and Greening has implemented the Trial Measures for Promoting Inbound Tourism in Suzhou, actively promoting more convenient payment and park entry for overseas visitors. As of July 22, the 14 directly-administered classical garden scenic areas including the Humble Administrator’s Garden, Lingering Garden, Tiger Hill, and Lion Grove Forest received a total of 143,200 foreign tourists, representing a year-on-year increase of 181.89%.

To attract foreign visitors, Suzhou’s garden scenic areas have launched a series of convenience measures. Except for the Humble Administrator’s Garden, Lingering Garden, and Lion Grove Forest, all other gardens have canceled real-name reservation requirements, allowing foreign tourists to scan a code and purchase tickets for direct entry. To improve payment experiences, POS machines for overseas bank cards have been installed in all key 4A-level and above garden scenic areas, adding overseas card payment services. Scenic areas have opened on-site comprehensive service counters: foreign tourists encountering difficulties with self-service ticketing machines or other channels can seek staff assistance for on-site ticket purchase, ensuring smooth entry in all situations.

Entry procedures for foreign tourist groups have been simplified. After the tour guide completes entry formalities, the group only needs to scan a group QR code for verification to enter directly. In addition, the Suzhou Gardens ticketing channel has been integrated into the overseas version of Ctrip’s App, enabling foreigners to buy tickets and enter directly via Ctrip.

To further streamline ticketing for foreign visitors, the Bureau will roll out more effective measures in the second half of this year. For gardens that still require real-name reservations, currently only passports are accepted; the Bureau will add ticketing channels for permanent residence permits and Hong Kong and Macau travel permits. The self-operated ticketing platform interface will be fully upgraded, and an English version of the Suzhou Gardens Tourism Website will be developed. Foreign tourists can also purchase tickets through the exclusive overseas payment channel for Suzhou Gardens on Alipay.

Furthermore, the Bureau will standardize and update foreign-language signs around scenic areas, produce bilingual brochures for the gardens, and improve electronic bilingual audio guide services inside the scenic areas, helping foreign tourists better understand scenic information and enhance their overall visiting experience.
