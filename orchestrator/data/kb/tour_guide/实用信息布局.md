为进一步提升游客游览体验，擦亮世界文化遗产金字招牌，拙政园管理处近日正式启动“一馆五园”指示牌优化工作。此次优化从设计美学、导览功能、长效维护三个维度全面提升服务质量，将传统园林服务与现代科技深度融合，让游客在感受古典园林魅力的同时，享受更便捷、更具人文关怀的游览体验。

设计美学彰显园林韵味。作为园林景观的重要组成部分，每一块指示牌都经过精心设计，深度融入江南园林美学精髓。在保留实用功能的同时，采用与建筑相协调的材质和色调，使指示牌成为传播园林文化的载体。此外，管理处结合留听阁、四面厅的围挡搭建与修缮工作，将施工围挡转化为知识传播窗口，通过展板呈现传统油漆工艺、修缮流程等内容，让施工期成为游客了解古建保护的珍贵契机。

精准指引赋能游览体验。重点厅堂配备中英文双语解说牌，并在全园指示牌中嵌入二维码。游客扫码即可畅听专业景点讲解、品读园林历史故事。通过“有形标识+数字服务”的模式，为游客提供更便捷、更富趣味性的游览体验。

长效维护保障服务品质。管理处建立健全"日巡、周护、月检"三级维护制度，每日安排专人巡检，修复破损、褪色标识；每周开展深度清洁保养，确保指示牌常新常亮；每月核查景点名称、游览路线、英文用语等基础信息，确保准确无误。此次升级还特别关注特殊群体需求，通过指示牌清晰标注无障碍通道与卫生间位置，确保每位游客都能畅享园林之美，彰显世界文化遗产地的包容与温度。

From "Guidance" to "Shared Appreciation" — Humble Administrator’s Garden Upgrades Navigation Services

Source: Humble Administrator’s Garden Administration (Suzhou Garden Museum) Date: July 15, 2025

To further enhance visitor experience and uphold the prestige of the World Heritage Site, the Humble Administrator’s Garden Administration has officially launched an upgrade to its “One Museum, Five Gardens” signage system. The project improves service quality across three dimensions: aesthetic design, navigation functionality, and long-term maintenance, deeply integrating traditional garden services with modern technology. This allows visitors to appreciate classical garden charm while enjoying more convenient and human-centered experiences.

Aesthetic Design Embodies Garden Charm

As an integral part of the landscape, every sign is carefully designed to embody the essence of Jiangnan garden aesthetics. While retaining practical functions, materials and colors harmonizing with the architecture are used, turning signs into carriers of cultural communication. In addition, in conjunction with the construction and restoration of the Listening to Rain Pavilion and Four-Sided Hall, construction barriers are transformed into educational displays. Traditional painting techniques and restoration processes are presented on panels, turning the renovation period into a valuable opportunity for visitors to learn about ancient architecture conservation.

Precise Guidance Empowers Visitor Experience

Bilingual (Chinese-English) interpretation signs are installed at major halls, and QR codes are embedded in all garden signs. Visitors may scan the codes to access professional audio explanations and historical stories. This “physical signage + digital service” model delivers a more convenient and engaging experience.

Long-Term Maintenance Ensures Service Quality

A three-level maintenance system is established: daily inspection, weekly care, and monthly assessment. Special staff inspect and repair damaged or faded signs daily; conduct thorough cleaning weekly to keep signs in good condition; and verify place names, routes, and English translations monthly to ensure accuracy. The upgrade also prioritizes accessibility: barrier-free routes and restroom locations are clearly marked, ensuring all visitors can enjoy the garden, reflecting the inclusivity and warmth of a World Heritage Site.

为了营造安全有序、和谐优美的游览参观环境，我管理处依照国家、省、市有关法律法规，特制定本须知，希望您在游览和参观过程中遵守相关事项，服从现场管理，做到安全游览、文明参观。

一、请您积极配合，避免下列行为，共同维护好安全、文明的游览环境

1.禁止携带下列物品入园（馆）：危险品（枪支弹药、易燃易爆品、管制刀具、化学制剂等）；宠物（符合规定的导盲犬除外）；车辆（老幼病残者专用的非机动车除外）等。

2.禁止在园（馆）内进行下列不安全不文明行为和违法违规行为：吸烟（含电子烟）、饮酒（含酒精饮料）、使用明火、升放无人机、攀爬古建假山、翻越围栏、进入危险及非开放区域、损坏文物、破坏展品、毁坏景观、损害古树名木、攀摘花果、喂食动物、丢弃杂物、悬拉横幅摄影、制造噪音、游泳垂钓、宿营野餐、随意躺卧、着装不整、吐痰便溺、运动游戏、婚纱摄影、兜售商品、散发广告、揽客讲解、封建迷信、聚众赌博、哄闹斗殴、非法集会等。

未经相关部门批准，禁止在园（馆）内进行电影电视拍摄和宣传推广活动。

3.发现上述物品或行为以及法律法规禁止的其他行为，我处将到场进行管控。对不接受劝阻或造成损失的，我处将依照法律法规给予处罚，涉及违法犯罪的，我处将联系相关部门处理。

二、请您遵守下列安全游览、文明参观的提示事项

1.请遵守园（馆）内各类安全警示标识规定，服从现场安全和秩序管理要求；

2.请遵守文明旅游行为规范，爱护旅游资源，保护生态环境；

3.请照看好老人、儿童、残障人士，谨防走失；

4.请保管好个人财物，避免遗失；

5.园内池塘较多，请勿靠近，小心落水；

6.园内假山较多，请勿攀爬，小心碰头；

7.园内路面、台阶雨雪湿滑，小心滑倒；

8.雷暴天气下，请勿靠近高大树木，避免雷击；

9.全园视频监控，注意文明言行；

10.全园保持清洁，请勿乱扔垃圾。

Visitor Regulations for the Humble Administrator’s Garden (Suzhou Garden Museum)

Source: Suzhou Municipal Bureau of Gardens and Greening Date: August 19, 2025

To maintain a safe, orderly, and pleasant visiting environment, the Administrative Office has formulated these regulations in accordance with national, provincial, and municipal laws and regulations. Visitors are required to comply with these rules and follow on-site management to ensure safe and civilized visits.

I. Prohibited Items and Behaviors

Prohibited items: Dangerous goods (firearms, ammunition, explosives, flammable materials, controlled knives, chemicals, etc.); pets (except certified guide dogs); vehicles (except non-motorized vehicles for the elderly, infirm, sick, and disabled).

Prohibited activities: Smoking (including e-cigarettes), drinking alcohol, using open flames, flying drones, climbing ancient buildings or rockeries, crossing fences, entering restricted or dangerous areas, damaging cultural relics or exhibits, destroying landscapes or ancient trees, picking flowers or fruits, feeding animals, littering, hanging banners for photography, making noise, swimming, fishing, camping, picnicking, lying down randomly, improper dressing, spitting, urinating in public, playing sports, wedding photography, selling goods, distributing advertisements, unlicensed tour guiding, superstition, gambling, fighting, illegal assemblies, etc. Without official approval, filming, TV shooting, and promotional activities are forbidden.

Any violation will be stopped immediately. Violators who refuse to cooperate or cause damage will be punished in accordance with laws and regulations. Criminal offenses will be referred to relevant authorities.

II. Safety and Courtesy Guidelines

Obey all safety signs and on-site management.

Respect cultural heritage and protect the ecological environment.

Take care of the elderly, children, and people with disabilities to prevent loss.

Keep your personal belongings safe.

Stay away from ponds to avoid drowning.

Do not climb rockeries to prevent injury.

Be careful of slippery paths and steps in rain or snow.

Keep away from tall trees during thunderstorms to avoid lightning.

The garden is under video surveillance; please behave politely.

Keep the garden clean; do not litter.

III. Legal Basis

These regulations are formulated under: Cultural Relics Protection Law of the People’s Republic of China; Regulations on the Implementation of the Cultural Relics Protection Law; Tourism Law of the People’s Republic of China; Tourism Safety Management Measures; Fire Protection Law of the People’s Republic of China; World Cultural Heritage Protection and Management Measures; Public Security Administration Punishments Regulations; Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Public Place Health Administration Regulations; Suzhou Garden Protection and Management Regulations; Suzhou Dog Management Regulations; Notice on Strengthening UAV Management for Public Safety, etc.
